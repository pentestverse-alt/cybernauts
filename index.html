Step 02 — Detection pipeline

What happens under the hood

Every scan runs the same four-stage pipeline before a verdict is issued.

PARSE

Header extraction

From, Reply-To, Return-Path, Received chain and Authentication-Results are pulled and normalized.

VERIFY

Auth & identity check

SPF, DKIM and DMARC results are validated; display-name vs domain mismatches are flagged.

CROSS-CHECK

Reputation lookup

Sender IP and embedded links are checked against live threat intelligence feeds.

SCORE

Forensic report

Weighted signals produce a 0–100 score, a verdict, and a plain-language "why flagged" list.
